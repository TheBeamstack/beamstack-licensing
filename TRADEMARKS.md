# Beamstack Trademark Policy

**v1.0 · 2026-08-30 · Contact: askdaoudi@gmail.com**

This policy governs use of the Beamstack marks. It is **separate from, and
survives, every software licence** in the Beamstack License Framework — including
the open-source tiers, where the licence itself grants no trademark rights. It is
the mechanism that keeps the brand meaningful when the code is freely forkable.

> Not legal advice. To be reviewed by a lawyer and re-issued on Beamstack's
> incorporation. Pending incorporation, the mark owner of record is Abdellah
> Daoudi (sole proprietor, Kingdom of Morocco).

## 1. The marks

- **"Beamstack"** — word mark, **registered with OMPIC** (Office Marocain de la
  Propriété Industrielle et Commerciale, Morocco).
- **The Beamstack logo** — the figurative mark and the "beam"/"stack" wordmark
  **as published at https://beam-stack.com**. That URL, not this document, is the
  operative reference: the same construction BCL-1.1 §1.17(b) uses to define the
  Attribution Mark. The current form is described in §1.1, and the mark's history
  is recorded there because the definition follows the site and the site changes.
- **Product names** — "Bunyan", "Road-Designer", "Miqdar", "Maître d'Ouvrage",
  "Planitor", "BIMsync", and other names Beamstack applies to its products.

Collectively, "the Beamstack Marks".

## 2. What no licence grants

No licence in any BLF tier (AGPL, MPL, Apache, BCL, the Commercial EULA, the
Dedicated Agreement) grants any right in the Beamstack Marks, except the narrow,
mandatory attribution use that BCL §3.7 requires of Community-tier deployments.

## 3. What you may do without permission (nominative use)

- State accurately that your software **"is based on"**, **"is a fork of"**, or
  **"is compatible with"** a Beamstack product.
- Use a product name in prose, documentation, articles, comparisons, and talks,
  to refer to the genuine Beamstack product.
- For **Community-tier** software, display the "Powered by Beamstack" attribution
  exactly as BCL §3.7 requires (that clause is your permission for that use).
- Keep the Beamstack Marks **unaltered** where a licence requires you to preserve
  notices.

## 4. What you may not do without a written agreement

- **Name your own product, service, company, or domain** using "Beamstack" or a
  Beamstack product name, or anything confusingly similar (e.g. "BeamstackPro",
  "Bunyan Cloud", "beamstack-hosting.com").
- Run a **fork** under the original Beamstack product name. A fork **must be
  renamed** — pick your own name and you may say it is "a fork of Bunyan".
- Use the Beamstack logo as **your** logo, or in a way that implies Beamstack
  endorses, sponsors, or is affiliated with your product, event, or company.
- **Modify, recolour, redraw, or animate** the Beamstack logo.
- Use the Beamstack Marks on **merchandise**, or register them (or confusingly
  similar marks) as a trademark or domain in any jurisdiction.

## 5. Forks — the practical rule

You may fork any BLF-Open or BLF-Community project as the licence allows. When
you distribute or deploy your fork:

- **rename it** — new product name, new logo, remove "Beamstack" from the name;
- you **may** (BLF-Open) or **must** (BLF-Community, per BCL §3.7) keep a factual
  "based on Beamstack software" credit;
- update the copyright notices to add your own; do **not** remove Beamstack's.

## 6. Enforcement

Beamstack may require anyone breaching this policy to stop using the Beamstack
Marks and to rename. Trademark rights are enforced independently of, and in
addition to, any software-licence remedy.
